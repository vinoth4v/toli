## Reading order

Read in sequence for the full argument. If you are short on time:

- **Founders / investors:** Part 1, then §11 Roadmap and §12 Team & budget in Part 5.
- **Engineering lead:** Parts 2 and 3.
- **Finance / legal:** Part 4 — particularly §8.3 (GST classification) and §8.4 (aggregator licensing), both of which need professional advice before code is written.
- **Ops / growth:** §2 in Part 1, and Part 5 in full.
